Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

One-time projects have a defined scope. Monthly services begin with a three-month minimum commitment, billed monthly, then continue month-to-month. Your proposal and service agreement outline the scope and cancellation terms.

Do your monthly packages include content creation?

Yes. Monthly management includes content strategy, captions, graphics, video editing from client-provided footage, and publishing within the agreed scope. On-site filming and professional photography are quoted separately.

Do I need to provide photos and videos?

For most monthly management packages, you provide raw photos and video footage for us to transform into polished content. Additional content-production services can be discussed if needed.

Are paid ads included?

No. Paid advertising and ad spend are separate from organic social media management.

Can you work with my existing website and social accounts?

Yes. We can improve your current digital presence rather than requiring you to start over. Growth Foundation includes website messaging recommendations; website development is not included.

What platforms do you manage?

Services can include Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and other short-form platforms depending on your audience and strategy. Your proposal confirms the platforms and distribution included.

How quickly can I expect results?

Social media growth is cumulative. The initial focus is building a stronger brand presence, consistent content and a clear strategy; performance is then monitored and optimized over time.

Can I start with a one-time project before monthly management?

Yes. Brand & Social Reset or Growth Foundation are good options if you are not ready for ongoing management.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. Businesses with larger content needs, multiple locations, paid campaigns or broader marketing support can receive a custom proposal.
